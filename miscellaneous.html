More About Me

Tennis

I picked up tennis in 2019 and have been an enthusiastic amateur ever since. It's one of my favorite ways to unwind. There is nothing quite as satisfying as hitting a powerful forehand that paints the line or, on a good day, a clean backhand. I have also come to enjoy watching tennis matches, with Iga Świątek and Karolína Muchová among my current favorite players. I greatly admire the mental strength tennis requires: staying fully present and never giving up until the final point.

Cooking & Food

I love being adventurous in the kitchen. As an operations researcher, I naturally approach cooking as a multi-objective optimization problem. I try to balance nutrient and time constraints, schedule parallel tasks, and experiment with diverse cuisines and cooking techniques to find the Pareto frontier of effort and taste. You can check out my ongoing recipe log of some interesting combinations. When not cooking, my husband and I enjoy exploring Toronto’s vibrant restaurant scene and discovering food from around the world when we travel.

Early Life

I grew up in Macheng (麻城), a beautiful city in central China, famous for its azalea blossoms. I later attended Wuhan No. 2 High School, where my love for rigorous problem-solving was first ignited through Math Olympiad training, eventually leading me to my undergrad in math at USTC, then Berkeley, and now to UofT.

Rocky the Cat

Rocky brings countless small happy moments to our daily life. Rocky is exceptionally sweet, genuinely funny, incredibly soft, and always comforting to hug. Born in February 2014 in Florida, he spent his early years as a working Ragdoll "cat dad" at a cattery in northern California. I adopted him in April 2021, shortly after his official retirement. These days, he strictly optimizes his daily schedule for two objectives: maximizing snack intake and sleeping. You can find his pictures on @rocky.themittedragdoll.
